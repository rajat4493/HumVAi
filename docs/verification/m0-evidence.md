## What the tests actually cover
- Determinism (same actions ⇒ identical outcome and state hash); initialization; invalid challenge rejection.
- Partial observability (no hidden-rule keys in observations or step results); ASCII observation snapshot.
- Movement/budget: bump costs an action; exact-budget loss; win on last action; actions after game end rejected and flagged in replay; INTERACT range; legal actions.
- Per-level rules: L2 real vs decoy switch and 6 optimal solutions; L3 plate/block/door; L4 jammed switch and override; L5 tripwire/epoch/inversion plus naive (18) vs optimal (10) paths and a losing path; RESET semantics.
- Solvability and within-budget for every level; replayed optimal solution wins in exactly the optimal length.
- E2E: Welcome → Briefing → play level 1 by keyboard (desktop) and D-pad (mobile) to victory in exactly 8 actions; Try again resets to 0; no console errors; holding a key = 1 action; no horizontal scroll.

## Not verified / not measured
- Frame rate, load time, memory on real devices (screenshots ran under software GL).
- Real-user comprehension of controls or of any level (no playtests).
- Touch swipe gesture (implemented, not e2e-tested); D-pad is tested.
- Safari/Firefox (Chromium only).
- Screen-reader experience, colour-blind simulation, audio (not built).
- Level difficulty against an actual LLM (no AI integration in M0).
